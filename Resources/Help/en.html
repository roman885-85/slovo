Getting started

  1. Where to keep the app. Keep “Слово.app” in the Applications folder: from there an update can replace the app. To install Slovo on another Mac, use the guide on GitHub — open or forward it from this computer, since this help is not available on a Mac where the app has not started yet.
  2. Permissions. When the system asks to “find devices on your local network”, click “Allow”: the remote, the tablet and NDI need it. Other permissions (screen recording, folder access) are requested only when they are really needed — see “Troubleshooting”.
  3. Translations and songbooks. On the first launch Slovo offers to download translations, songbooks, backgrounds and templates. Later — Setup → Resources from GitHub…, or File → Import modules, templates and backgrounds… for your own files.
  4. Projector. Connect a projector or a second monitor — the slide goes there by itself. To choose another monitor: Setup → Settings… → General → Monitor for the slide. With a single monitor the slide is shown in its own window.
  5. Language. The Language/Язык menu: Українська, English, Русский, Deutsch.

Hover the mouse over any button and wait a moment — a tip tells what it does.

The main window

  • Mode tabs at the top: Bible, Songs, Presentation, Media, Pictures, Screen, Text. The slider to their right sets the list font size (or Ctrl + mouse wheel).
  • Work area — the content of the chosen tab: books and verses, songs, pages, files.
  • Bottom row (drag it up to make it taller):
    • Plan — the order of the service;
    • History — everything that has already been in the hall;
    • Preview — what is prepared but not yet in the hall;
    • Live screen — exactly what people see; the pointer and zoom work on it;
    • Control — page buttons, “Show”, “Hide”, the “Active” checkbox, slide template and backgrounds.
  • Drag the dividers to change the width of the Plan, the History and the live screen; double-click a divider to return the default width.

Showing in the hall

One rule for every tab: a single click prepares (a verse, song part or page appears in the preview), a double click, Enter or “Show” puts it in the hall. Switching tabs does not touch the hall: the slide stays as it was shown, and another tab’s template (for example, the song template) appears together with a slide shown from that tab.

Arrow keys and the “Active” checkbox

  • “Active” on (default): ↑ ↓ move to the next verse, song part or page right in the hall; ← → — only in the preview. Holding Ctrl swaps the pairs.
  • “Active” off: no arrow key changes the hall — the position moves only in the preview, and the slide goes to the hall with “Show” or Enter. Handy for finding the right place calmly while the previous slide stays on the wall.
  • Shift with an arrow extends the selection to the next verse; Ctrl+A puts the whole chapter on one slide.
  • A presenter clicker (Logitech, Kensington and others) always turns the hall, whatever “Active” says: PageDown — next, PageUp — back, the blackout button — black screen.

Bible

  • Choose a translation, book, chapter and verse. Books can be shown as tiles or as a list (buttons next to the book list).
  • Quick selection (F4): type a reference — “John 3:16”, “Gen 1:1-5”, “1 Cor 13” (in the book names of the translation). Separately: F7 — book, F8 — chapter, F9 — verse by number or by words of the text.
  • Search (F3) — by words across the whole translation; Ctrl+F3 shows or hides the results.
  • Several verses: click with Shift or use Shift+arrow. A double click on the selection shows them together.
  • Two translations on a slide — the “One translation / Two translations” button in the translation strip; the second translation is chosen by right-clicking its tab. Verse numbering between translations is matched in Setup → Bible translation numbering discrepancy editor.
  • A long verse is split into pages by itself: the arrow turns the pages first, then moves to the next verse.
  • Right-click a verse: “Add to Plan”, “Copy to the Text tab”, “Copy to clipboard”.

Songs

  • Choose a songbook and a song: search by number, title or words of the text.
  • The parts of the song (verses, chorus, bridge) are below. A click prepares a part, a double click puts it in the hall; the arrows turn the parts.
  • Right-click a song or a part — “Add to Plan”.
  • Song editor: new song, text editing, groups, order, part colours. Songbook import — VisioBible .vbm, .songbook, SoftProjector .sps, plain text .txt.

Backing track

  • The backing track panel is right here, above the songbook tabs, across the whole window. Drag the edge above it to make the panel taller or shorter; double-click that edge to restore it.
  • It has its own list: music dropped onto the panel goes there, not among the player’s intros and videos. Double-click a track to play it at once.
  • The waveform on the dark card shows the whole track and where the sound is now; click or drag on it to play from that point. Next to it is a level meter for the left and right channels (red means clipping).
  • Buttons: play, pause, stop (back to the start), loop and “then the next one” (keeps playing the list).
  • Key: “♭ −0.5” and “♯ +0.5” lower or raise the key by a semitone (up to ±3 tones) without changing the tempo; click “Key: …” to return to the original. The key is remembered for each file.
  • A backing track plays separately from the show: you can turn song parts or switch tabs and the music keeps playing.

Presentations and pictures

  • Presentation: “+” adds a PDF or PowerPoint file (.pptx, .ppsx and others); the file list and page thumbnails are on the left. The arrows and the ‹ › buttons turn the pages.
  • Pictures: “+” adds pictures or a whole folder. You can also drop files onto the window.
  • Slideshow: the “Slideshow” button and an interval in seconds — pages turn by themselves, in a loop. Turning a page by hand does not break the count.

Media: intros, video, streams

  • Open a file (video or audio), a network stream or a YouTube link.
  • Player buttons: to the beginning, rewind, play/pause, stop, fast forward, to the end, repeat, mute, audio track. The “to screen” switch decides whether the video goes to the hall.
  • Ctrl+M — media player, Ctrl+P — play/pause.
  • Backing tracks have moved to the Songs tab — the panel above the songbook tabs.

Screen

Shows a monitor or a window of another program live in the hall and in NDI. Choose the source and click “Show”. The first time, macOS asks for the Screen & System Audio Recording permission — see “Troubleshooting”. “Refresh the list” helps when a window you just opened is not listed yet.

Text (announcements)

Type a title and the text of an announcement — they appear in the preview; to the hall — “Show” or F5. The buttons next to the title: “Add to plan” and “Clear text”. A Bible verse can be copied here with a right click.

Plan and History

  • The Plan is the order of the service. Add items with a right click (“Add to Plan”) in the Bible, songs and search results, and announcements with the button on the Text tab.
  • A click on a Plan item prepares it in the preview, a double click or Enter puts it in the hall. F2 moves to the Plan.
  • Plan buttons: clear, open, save, delete the line, move up, move down. Items can be dragged.
  • The History records everything that was in the hall. It works like the Plan: click — preview, double click or Enter — hall. Showing an item from the History again does not change the History.
  • A sermon plan from the preacher’s tablet can become the main Plan with one click, and the service plan can be brought back.

Templates, backgrounds and the constructor

  • The Control panel has three thumbnails: slide template, slide background and common background. Click a thumbnail to choose; right-click the common background to turn it on, off or remove it.
  • Ctrl+B — choose the common background, Ctrl+Alt+B — the slide background.
  • Setup → Slide constructor — separate editors for the Bible and for songs: fonts, colours, shadows, outline, margins, background, text placement. Changes are visible at once.
  • Setup → Web slide editor… — pages for OBS and lobby monitors.

Where the data lives

Everything of yours — translations, songbooks, backgrounds, templates, fonts, service plans, settings and history — lives outside the program, in the folder:

{{DATA}}

  • To see it from the program: Settings… → Paths — the full address is there, with “Show in Finder” and “Copy the path”.
  • An update replaces only the app. The data folder stays where it is, and macOS does not ask for permissions again — screen recording and local network keep working.
  • To move everything to another computer: copy this folder (and the program itself).
  • To keep a spare copy: copy this folder — it holds everything you have downloaded and set up.
  • The app package itself keeps only the defaults: Slovo.ini, hotkeys.ini, the help and the numbering rules.

Projector, NDI, pointer and zoom

  • Projector — the monitor is chosen in Settings… → General.
  • NDI for streaming (OBS, vMix) — in Settings… → Media: frame size, transparent background, sound. Sound of streams and YouTube in NDI needs the “Screen & System Audio Recording” permission.
  • Pointer: press the left mouse button on the live screen and move without releasing. Spot colour, size and brightness are in the bar under the live screen, together with where the pointer goes (everywhere, hall only or NDI only).
  • Zoom: turn on “Zoom” and click the live screen — that part of the page fills the wall. “Centre” brings the view back to the middle.
  • F11 — save a snapshot of the slide to a file.

Remote, tablet and browser

  • The device must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the computer.
  • Browser remote: Setup → Browser remote… — the address and a QR code. It opens on any phone, tablet or computer.
  • Android apps — “Slovo Remote” and “Slovo Tablet”: Setup → Android apps…, installed by QR code.
  • Password (PIN), ports and switching on — Settings… → Remote API.
  • If the remote cannot find the computer — see “Troubleshooting”.

Updates

  • The app checks for updates on every launch, every 30 minutes and whenever you return to it, and tells you about a new version; until it is installed, an “Update …” button stays at the right of the top row. To check by hand: Setup → Check for program update… or Help → About → Check for updates.
  • The update shows a progress bar and the current step, then the app restarts. Translations, songbooks and settings stay.
  • So that the update can replace the app, keep “Слово.app” in the Applications folder, not in Downloads.

Troubleshooting

macOS permissions

The app checks what is missing at launch and shows the Help → macOS permissions… window: the state of each permission and a button that opens the right section of System Settings. After an update macOS may ask for permissions again — this is normal.

  • Local Network — for the remote, the tablet and NDI. If you once clicked “Don’t Allow”: “System Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Local Network” → turn on “Слово”.
  • Incoming connections (firewall) — “Allow”, otherwise the remote cannot connect.
  • Screen Recording — for the Screen tab and stream sound in NDI: “Privacy & Security” → “Screen & System Audio Recording” → turn on “Слово”, then restart the app.
  • Folder access to Documents, Desktop, Downloads or a removable drive — “Allow” when you open files from there.

The remote or tablet cannot see the app

  • The computer and the device are on the same Wi-Fi network; a guest network or “client isolation” in the router gets in the way.
  • Check that the remote is on: Settings… → Remote API; the address and state are in Browser remote….
  • Try the numeric address instead of the name slovo.local.

The update does not install

  • “Read-only volume” — the app was started straight from Downloads or from a disk image. Move “Слово.app” to Applications and start it from there.

Other

  • The slide is on the wrong monitor — Settings… → General → Monitor.
  • Help → Diagnostics… checks the app and saves a report to a file — you can send it to the developer.
  • Questions and bugs — GitHub Issues.
